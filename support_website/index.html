Urja 24

Urja 24 is a revolutionary event management app that simplifies event discovery, registration, and entry with QR codes. Users can earn virtual coins by registering, attending events, and referring friends to the app, which can be used in the in-app store for exclusive event-related items. With gamification and interactive challenges, Urja 24 ensures users have a fantastic time at events. Website support is available for any inquiries or assistance.
